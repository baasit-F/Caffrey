Small on purpose

A quiet, family-run campground right on the shoreline.

There are no mega-resort crowds at Caffrey, just 29 sites along the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack off Route 590 in Lakeville. Pull in the RV or pitch a tent, launch the boat, grab bait at the camp store, and watch the sun go down behind the ridge.

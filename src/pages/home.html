Lakefront camping on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack

Caffrey Recreation Area is a family-run campground with RV, trailer and tent sites in Lakeville, PA, in the Pocono Mountains. Every site has 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring. The boat launch, gas dock and camp store are all a short walk from your site.

See the Campsites View Rates

One of Pennsylvania’s largest lakes is right outside your camper: fishing at first light, a day on the boat, and sunsets over the water from the west shore. Hawley’s shops and restaurants are a short drive away, but most guests never want to leave the shoreline.

Welcome to Caffrey

A small, family-run campground on the west shore of one of Pennsylvania’s largest lakes, where the water, the docks and the launch are all a short walk away.

The campsites

Small on purpose. Right on the shoreline.

No mega-resort crowds. Just 29 sites strung along the lake, each with 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring. Pull in the RV or pitch a tent, and the lake does the rest.

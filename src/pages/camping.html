Lakefront camping on Lake Wallenpaupack.

Caffrey Recreation Area has RV, trailer and tent sites on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack in Lakeville, PA. Every site has 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring.

The sites

Wooded sites a short walk from the water

Caffrey is small on purpose. Sites sit along the lake's west shore, so the water, the docks and the launch are all a short walk away. Bring a big rig, a pop-up or a couple of tents.

  • 30 amp electric hookup at every site
  • Water at every site
  • Picnic table and fire ring
  • RV and trailer dump station access
  • Up to 8 guests per site (4 included)
  • Coin-operated showers and laundry by the camp store

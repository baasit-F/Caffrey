The camper's guide to Lake Wallenpaupack.

Lake Wallenpaupack is a 5,700-acre lake in the Pocono Mountains of northeastern Pennsylvania, with about 52 miles of shoreline. It's known for boating, bass and striper fishing, and lakeside towns like Hawley. Caffrey Recreation Area sits on its west shore in Lakeville.

Things to do

How to spend a lake weekend

{{icon:fish}}

Fishing

The lake holds largemouth and smallmouth bass, striped bass, walleye, chain pickerel and yellow perch. Pick up bait and tackle at the Caffrey camp store before heading out. A Pennsylvania fishing license is required.

{{icon:boat}}

Boating & watersports

Wide-open water is good for tubing, waterskiing and cruising, and coves are good for a quiet paddle. Launch from Caffrey for $10 per stay.

{{icon:sun}}

Sunsets on the west shore

The west-shore location means evening light over the water. Grab a spot at the waterfront picnic area around golden hour.

{{icon:pin}}

Hawley

A short drive away, the town of Hawley has restaurants, antique shops and a historic silk mill.

{{icon:leaf}}

State parks & hiking

Promised Land State Park and the Lacawac Sanctuary are both nearby, with forest trails, more lakes and wildlife.

{{icon:calendar}}

Fall foliage

Late September into October brings peak color around the lake. The end of the camping season is one of the prettiest times to visit.

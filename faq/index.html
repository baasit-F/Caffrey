Rates & reservations

How much does camping cost at Caffrey Recreation Area?

RV and trailer sites are $52 per night regular, $58 weekend and $65 holiday. Tent sites are $40 regular, $46 weekend and $48 holiday. Every rate includes up to 4 people.

How much are extra guests?

Up to 4 additional guests can stay for $10 per guest per night, for a maximum of 8 guests per site. Children under 5 stay free.

How do I make a reservation?

Call (570) 226-4608 or email Reservations@caffreyrecreation.com with your dates, site type and number of guests. A deposit holds your site.

How much is the deposit?

Non-holiday reservations need a one-night deposit per site. Holiday reservations need a three-night deposit per site.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel 15 or more days before arrival for a refund minus a $25 administration fee. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit. There are no refunds for weather or early departures.

Will my price change if rates go up?

No. Reservations made before a price change keep their original price.

Sites & amenities

Are the campsites on the water?

Yes. Caffrey has 29 campsites along the west shoreline of Lake Wallenpaupack, plus a waterfront picnic area.

What hookups do the sites have?

Every site has 30 amp electric and water, a picnic table and a fire ring. RV and trailer campers also get access to the dump station.

Can I bring a tent?

Yes. Tent sites allow up to 2 tents per site and include electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring.

Are there showers and laundry?

Yes. There are restrooms with hot showers and on-site laundry facilities.

What does the camp store sell?

Basic camping and fishing supplies, bait, firewood, ice, cold drinks, snacks and ice cream.

Are pets allowed?

No. Pets are not allowed at Caffrey Recreation Area.

Boating

Can I launch my boat at the campground?

Yes. Campers can store and launch a boat for $10 for their entire stay.

Is there gas for boats?

Yes. Caffrey has an on-site gas dock on Lake Wallenpaupack.

Where can I buy or service a boat nearby?

Capri Marine, the full-service marina next door at 457 Lakeshore Drive, handles boat sales, service and storage.

Getting here & hours

Where is Caffrey Recreation Area?

431 Lakeshore Drive, Lakeville, PA 18438, on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack in Wayne County, off Route 590 in the Pocono Mountains.

What are the office and store hours?

Thursday and Sunday 9 AM to 5 PM, Friday and Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, closed Monday through Wednesday. Call ahead if bad weather is expected.

When is the camping season?

The season generally runs from late spring through mid-October. Call (570) 226-4608 to confirm this year's opening and closing dates.

How far is Lake Wallenpaupack from New York City and Philadelphia?

Lake Wallenpaupack is roughly a 2 to 2.5 hour drive from New York City and about 2.5 hours from Philadelphia, depending on traffic.

Rates & policies

Camping rates

RV and trailer sites at Caffrey cost $52 per night regular, $58 weekend and $65 holiday. Tent sites cost $40, $46 and $48. Rates include up to 4 people, and extra guests are $10 per night.

Deposits & cancellations
Deposit to reserve
One night per site for regular stays. Three nights per site for holiday stays.
Cancel 15+ days before arrival
Your deposit is refunded minus a $25 administration fee.
Cancel within 14 days
The deposit is forfeited (one night, or three nights for holidays).
Weather & early departure
No refunds for weather-related cancellations or leaving early.
Campground rules
Guests
Maximum of 8 guests per site.
Pets
Pets are not allowed.
Tents
Up to 2 tents per tent site.
Check-in & check-out
Check-in 1 PM, check-out 12 PM. Sites must be set up before 11 PM.
Quiet hours
10 PM to 7 AM daily.
Vehicles
One camping unit and one vehicle per site. Extra vehicles park in a designated area for a fee.
Not permitted
Swimming, alcohol, smoking or vaping, fireworks, outside firewood, golf carts, ATVs and dirt bikes.
Electric
Not provided for air conditioners or heaters.
Boats & trailers
Must be registered and parked in the designated area, not at your site. Boats use the pump-out station at the dock.

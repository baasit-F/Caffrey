Lakefront camping on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack

Caffrey Recreation Area is a family-run campground with 29 waterfront RV, trailer and tent sites in Lakeville, PA, in the Pocono Mountains. Every site has 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring. The boat launch, gas dock and camp store are all a short walk from your site.

See the Campsites View Rates

One of Pennsylvania’s largest lakes is right outside your camper: bass and walleye at first light, a swim in the afternoon, and sunsets over the water from the west shore. Hawley’s shops and restaurants are a short drive away, but most guests never want to leave the shoreline.

Good to know

What does a night cost?

RV and trailer sites are $52, or $58 on weekends. Tent sites are $40, or $46 on weekends. Four guests are included.

Can I bring my boat?

Yes. $10 covers launching and storage for your whole stay, and there’s a gas dock right on site.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets aren’t permitted. Each site holds up to 8 guests, and kids under 5 stay free.

Where the campsite meets the shoreline.

Lakefront Sites.

Twenty-nine campsites strung along the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack, one of Pennsylvania’s largest lakes. Every one has 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring. Small on purpose, and family-run.

Launch and store your boat for $10 for the whole stay, fuel up at the on-site gas dock, and grab bait, ice and firewood at the camp store. Your boat ties up a short walk from your fire ring.

Before you ask.

All questions
PRICING

What does a night cost?

RV and trailer sites are $52, or $58 on weekends. Tent sites are $40, or $46 on weekends. Four guests are included.

BOATING

Can I launch my boat?

Yes. $10 covers launching and storage for your whole stay, and there’s a gas dock on site.

POLICIES

Are pets allowed?

No, pets aren’t permitted. Sites hold up to 8 guests, and kids under 5 stay free.

Season fills fast

Holiday weekends go first.

A one-night deposit holds your site, or three nights on holidays. Cancel 15 or more days out for a refund less a $25 fee.

The campground

Small on purpose. Right on the shoreline.

Caffrey is a quiet, family-run campground on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack, just off Route 590 in Lakeville. No mega-resort crowds — just 29 sites strung along the water.

Pull in your RV or pitch a tent, launch the boat, grab ice and bait at the camp store, and spend the evening at the waterfront picnic area watching the sun drop behind the ridge.

Good questions

How much is camping on Lake Wallenpaupack here?

RV/trailer sites are $52 regular, $58 weekend and $65 holiday per night. Tent sites are $40, $46 and $48. Up to 4 people included.

Can I launch my own boat?

Yes — campers can store and launch a boat for $10 for their entire stay, and there's a gas dock on site.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets aren't permitted at Caffrey Recreation Area.

When is the campground open?

The camping season typically runs spring through mid-October. Call (570) 226-4608 for this year's dates.

How do I make a reservation?

Call (570) 226-4608 or email Reservations@caffreyrecreation.com. A one-night deposit holds your site (three nights for holidays).

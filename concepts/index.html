Caffrey Recreation Area — redesign concepts

Three directions for caffreyrecreation.com. Each has the same real content (rates, hours, amenities) plus SEO/AEO structured data. Pick one, or mix pieces from each.

Concept A

Lakeside Editorial

Full-bleed lake photography, serif type, booking bar up top. Calm, premium, trustworthy. Best fit for families and RV regulars.
Concept B

Retro Camp Poster

Illustrated national-park poster style with a clickable 29-site map. Memorable, very shareable, doesn't depend on photo quality.
Concept C

Trip Planner

App-style bento layout: live price calculator, "open now" status, quick answers written for AI search. Most conversion-focused.

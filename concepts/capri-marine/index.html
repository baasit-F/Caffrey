    Common questions

    What boat brands does Capri Marine sell?

    Avalon luxury pontoons, Aura pontoons and Chaparral sport and surf boats, plus a pre-owned inventory that changes through the season.

    Do you service Mercury engines?

    Yes. The service center has factory-authorized Mercury and MerCruiser technicians.

    Can I store my boat over the winter?

    Yes. We offer winter and dry dock storage, dry stack storage and seasonal boat slips. Call for availability and pricing.

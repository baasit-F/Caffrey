GPS address

Last few miles
The campground is off Route 590 on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack in Wayne County. Lakeshore Drive runs along the water.
Coming from NYC, NJ or Philly?
Most routes reach the lake via I-84. Allow about 2–2.5 hours from New York City and 2.5 hours from Philadelphia.

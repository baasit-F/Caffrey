How other campgrounds do it

Live homepages from comparable campgrounds, captured October 29, 2026. Closest fit to Caffrey first. Click a name to open the real site.

Closest fit: family campgrounds on the water

Similar price point and guests to Caffrey: families, RVs, boats, repeat summer visitors.

Best match

Lake George Escape

Family RV campground, Adirondacks NY

  • The whole screen is a real photo of guests tubing and kayaking.
  • Five menu items plus one orange Book Now button.
  • Plain and friendly, with no luxury styling. That suits a $52-a-night family campground.
Strong content

Ocean Lakes Family Campground

Family campground, Myrtle Beach SC

  • The first photo is an aerial shot of the campground and beach, so you see the whole place at once.
  • A search box asking "Plan Your Perfect Visit!"
  • Live webcams and clear Lodging, Rates and Amenities menus.
  • It's fun and colorful without looking cheap.
Booking pattern

KOA

National campground brand

  • The booking bar (where, check in, check out) sits directly on the photo.
  • Rotating photos of families at the site.
  • This is what campers are used to seeing when they book.

Local competition: Poconos

Direct competitor

Mountain Vista Campground

Pocono Mountains PA

  • The design is dated, but it has one great idea: short guest video clips like "My happy place" and "Generations of memories".
  • Caffrey could beat it with any clean site that has real photos.

Upscale: what AutoCamp is part of

These are luxury, $200+ a night brands. They look great, but the style doesn't really suit Caffrey's guests.

Upscale glamping

Firelight Camps

Glamping, Ithaca NY

  • Serif headline over a warm interior photo, with Availability Calendar and Book Now in the top corner.
Upscale cabins

Getaway (Outdoor Collection)

Cabin brand, now part of Marriott

  • One photo of a cabin at dusk and a single sentence.
  • Works because the photography is excellent.

What they all have in common

  • Real photos come first. Every one opens on a large photo of guests enjoying that place. Caffrey has 3 photos, and that's the main reason any design will feel flat.
  • Friendly, family-campground styling (Lake George, Ocean Lakes) suits Caffrey better than AutoCamp's luxury look.
  • One obvious Book Now button, plus a booking bar on the first screen, as KOA does.
  • Proof from real people: guest video clips, webcams, aerial views. A drone shot of the shoreline and the docks would do this for Caffrey.

Boating

Camp with your boat on Lake Wallenpaupack.

Campers at Caffrey can store and launch a boat for $10 for their entire stay, and fuel up at the on-site gas dock without leaving Lake Wallenpaupack.

Book Your StayCall (570) 226-4608

Launch & storage, $10

One flat fee covers launching and storing your boat for your whole stay, whether that's two nights or two weeks.

On-site gas dock

Top off on the water. There's no need to trailer out to a gas station.

Docks & slips

Wooden docks run along the shoreline, close to the campsites.

On the water

Room to run on one of PA's largest lakes

Lake Wallenpaupack has more than 50 miles of shoreline, so there's plenty to explore: coves for fishing, open water for tubing and skiing, and lakeside restaurants you can reach by boat.

  • Pennsylvania boat registration rules apply
  • Bait and tackle at the camp store
  • Waterfront picnic area for lunch breaks
Marina partner

Capri Marine

Shopping for a boat or need service? Capri Marine is a family-owned full-service marina next door, with Avalon, Aura and Chaparral boats, Mercury service, slips and storage.

Boating FAQ

Common questions

Can I launch my boat if I'm not camping?

Boat launching and storage are offered to campers for $10 per stay. Call (570) 226-4608 to ask about day launches.

Where do I fuel up?

Caffrey has its own gas dock on the lake, right at the campground.

Can I keep my boat in the water overnight?

Boat storage is included in the $10 per-stay fee. Call ahead to confirm dock space for your dates.

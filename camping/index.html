Camping

Lakefront camping on Lake Wallenpaupack.

Caffrey Recreation Area has 29 waterfront RV, trailer and tent sites on the west shore of Lake Wallenpaupack in Lakeville, PA. Every site has 30 amp electric, water, a picnic table and a fire ring.

Check availabilitySee rates
The sites

29 sites strung along the shoreline

Caffrey is small on purpose. Sites sit along the lake's west shore, so the water, the docks and the launch are all a short walk away. Bring a big rig, a pop-up or a couple of tents.

  • 30 amp electric hookup at every site
  • Water at every site
  • Picnic table and fire ring
  • RV and trailer dump station access
  • Up to 8 guests per site (4 included)

Good to know

Campground basics

No pets

Pets aren't permitted anywhere on the property.

8 guests max

Rates include 4 guests. Extra guests are $10 per night, and kids under 5 are free.

Weather

Call ahead if bad weather is expected. There are no weather refunds.
